## Methodology Notes

- **Embeddings:** Synthetic random unit-normalised float32 vectors (reproducible seed `0xDEADBEEF`).
- **LoCoMo corpus:** 20 sessions × 15 facts each (300 records). Ground truth = same-session facts.
- **LongMemEval corpus:** 15 topics × 4 sequential updates (60 records). Ground truth = latest update per topic.
- **Recall@K:** Fraction of ground-truth content strings found in top-K recalled results.
- **Latency:** `time.perf_counter()` wall-clock per `store.recall()` call; 10 warm-up queries discarded.
- **Context Reduction:** `100 × (1 − format_context_tokens / raw_haystack_tokens)` where token count ≈ `len(text) // 4`.
- **Write throughput (Run D):** `remember_batch()` in 500-record chunks; `remember()` for Runs A/B.
- **Competitor figures:** Indicative only, sourced from vendor documentation and public benchmarks.
